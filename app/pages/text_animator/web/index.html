Drag a key or its handles. Ctrl-click keys, or drag across empty space, to select several (Shift or Ctrl adds to them), then drag one to move them all. Hold Shift while dragging keys to move them only in time. Selected keys get a menu of their own. Double-click the curve to add a key; Delete removes the selected ones. Alt-drag a handle to break it from its partner. Drag along the time bar at the top to move the playhead. Click the channels above to show several at once, each in its own colour: keys of different channels can be selected together and moved as one, and a key dragged near another channel's key snaps to its time (hold Ctrl to drag freely). Right-click a channel to change its colour.
