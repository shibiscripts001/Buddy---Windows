Text Animator

Subtitles to Text+

Turns each subtitle on a subtitle track into its own Text+ clip on a video track, at the same time – ready to style, place and animate on the other tabs.

Titles are placed from a Text+ in your Media Pool, for exact timing. If the project has none, Buddy adds one the first time (a "Buddy Text+" bin) – nothing to set up. The video track suggested is the topmost empty one.

Apply position

Copies the position of each clip under the playhead onto every other Text+ clip on its video track, across the whole timeline. A track with nothing under the playhead is left alone.

Bounding

Two lines to drag in the preview (e.g. onto a safe zone's edges). Applying resizes every clip's text to fill the space between them and centres it there. Turning them on also stacks clips under the playhead together vertically.

Word preview For subtitles split one word per Text+ clip. Drag across empty space to select several words; Shift-click adds one.

Diagnostics

To help debug an animation: click the clip on the timeline, switch Resolve to the Fusion page, select the TextPlus node (or a modifier) in the Flow view, then dump it. Its full settings are saved to your Desktop as a JSON file you can share.
