Buddy Network

Chat with other Buddy users

A text chat for people who use Buddy, with a Global room and a Help room, rooms anyone can make, and end-to-end encrypted messages with your buddies. Other people see only the name you choose – never your IP address or anything else about you.

It's off until you turn it on: Buddy doesn't connect anywhere for it until then.
