        Sync everything

        Lines up every clip on the open timeline, places audio that has no timecode by its waveform, packs it onto the fewest tracks and pulls it to the start. You get one new timeline – the one you started from isn't touched.

        Sync by
        1

        Expand

        Every clip onto its own track, keeping position and trim. For moving clips around by hand.

        2

        Align

        Moves each clip to its timecode, relative to the earliest one. Clips with no timecode stay put.

        3

        Sync external audio

        Places lav packs and field recorders against the camera clips' scratch audio. Run it after Align. Needs ffmpeg.

        4

        Collapse

        Packs clips onto the fewest tracks. Only the track changes, never when a clip plays.

        5

        Shift

        Pulls everything to the start of the timeline.
